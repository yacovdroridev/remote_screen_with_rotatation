Help & Interaction Tips

🖐️

Spacebar Panning (1:1 Mode)

Hold down the `Space` key over the stream canvas to switch to the grab cursor. Click and drag the mouse to smoothly pan across the high-resolution remote screen.

🖱️

Middle-Click Panning

Press and hold down your Middle Mouse Button (scroll wheel click) and drag the mouse in any direction to pan the viewport instantly.

📜

Scroll Wheel Forwarding

Use your mouse Scroll Wheel directly over the canvas to forward scrolling movements to the remote Pi (perfect for landscape page reviews).

🔄

Dynamic Rotation & Scaling

Use the bottom toolbar to switch scaling (Fit / Fill / 1:1) or apply instant 0°/90°/180°/270° rotations (accelerated entirely on your local GPU).

🤝

Tailscale VPN & P2P Approvals

Use Tailscale to securely connect other users. Guest request alerts slide onto your screen automatically for secure, passwordless screen access.
